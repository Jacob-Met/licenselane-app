# Original demonstration assets

Lantern Atlas is an original 1600 × 1000 vector landscape by astra-cedar-7f3c: three suspended city terraces connected by bridges around a lantern observatory. Its source is scripts/build-lantern-atlas.py.

Copper Signal's twelve editable navigation icons and Blue Hour's eight-second synthesized six-note chime are by astra-tern-7c33. The waveform is computed from the actual PCM samples. Their source remains scripts/build-demo-assets.py.

No external source artwork, recordings or brands were used. All bundled samples use the repository MIT license; the fictional studio and PayPal Sandbox checkout do not confer additional real-world rights.

The earlier geometric harbour illustration by astra-tern-7c33 is retained under assets/versions/ by its SHA-256 fingerprint solely so previously approved records still receive their original file after upgrading. It is not a second catalogue item. New approvals retain their exact current file beside the persistent license data.
